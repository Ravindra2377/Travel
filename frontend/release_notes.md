# 🚀 CompareZ v1.0.0 — The "Aurora & Precision" Update

We are thrilled to announce a major overhaul of the CompareZ experience. This release brings a premium new design, a significantly smarter search engine, and a frictionless "one-tap" shopping experience.

---

## 🎨 Design & Experience (Aurora v2)
*   **Hero Section Redesign**: A stunning new gradient-driven hero section with interactive stats. Tap the **"5-in-1"** badge to jump straight to your store connections.
*   **Premium Deals Carousel**: "Trending Deals" has been transformed into a high-impact horizontal carousel featuring product photography and smart value tags like *"Hot Deal"* and *"Lowest Price."*
*   **Cleaner Layout**: Removed the redundant "Browse" section to maximize vertical space for price comparisons.
*   **Brand Polish**: Enhanced the "CompareZ" logo presence with improved typography and high-contrast white text for better readability on gradients.

## 🧠 Hyper-Accurate Search Engine
*   **Price-First Sorting**: By popular demand, results are now primarily sorted by **Lowest Price**. We've kept a 20-point relevance buffer so you always see exactly what you searched for, but at the best possible price.
*   **Unit & Brand Intelligence**: Improved matching logic that understands that "500g" is half of "1kg" and that "Coke" is the same as "Coca Cola."
*   **No More Ghost Results**: Fixed an issue where Flipkart would show "Trending" items even before you started your search.
*   **Parallel Scraping Optimization**: Enhanced resource blocking (ads/trackers) to make multi-platform searches up to 2 seconds faster.

## 📊 Live Progress Tracking
*   **Brand-Aware Badges**: Individual store badges now light up in their signature colors (Zepto Purple, Amazon Orange, etc.) as they finish scanning.
*   **Shimmering Loaders**: Added sleek progress bars and "pulsing" scanning indicators to keep you engaged while we find the best deals.
*   **Dynamic Saving Tips**: Added a rotating tip box to help you discover hidden savings while results load.

## 📲 Frictionless Connectivity
*   **One-Tap Buy**: Clicking on any "Trending Deal" now deep-links you directly into the corresponding store app (Zepto, Blinkit, etc.) or their website for a 2-second checkout.
*   **Legal Transparency**: Added a dedicated **Terms of Service** screen accessible from the Profile menu, ensuring full clarity on data accuracy and service limitations.
*   **Improved Navigation**: All sub-screens now feature premium custom headers with haptic-feedback back buttons.

## 🛠️ Stability & Infrastructure
*   **CI/CD Fixed**: Resolved the "slug mismatch" in `app.json` that was preventing GitHub Action builds from completing.
*   **Expo Go Compatibility**: Optimized configuration for easier wireless testing on physical iPhones.
*   **Bundling Optimization**: Resolved syntax errors and optimized module resolution for faster app startup.
