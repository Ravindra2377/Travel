CompareZ account and data deletion

Before you request deletion

Deleting your account is permanent. It can remove:

  • Your CompareZ account profile and login access.
  • Server-side search history associated with your account.
  • Connected-retailer session information held by CompareZ.
  • Other account-linked service data, subject to lawful retention.

Information stored only on your device, such as some wishlist or recent search data, may need to be cleared in the app or removed by uninstalling the app.

Submit your request

  1. Use the button below to email us from the same address used for your CompareZ account.
  2. Include the email address registered to the account.
  3. We may reply with an identity-verification step before processing the request.

Request account deletion

Do not include your password, retailer password, payment information, or authentication tokens.

Email deletion request

Processing and retention

We aim to review deletion requests within seven business days. Once verified, deletion will be completed within a reasonable period. We may retain limited records where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance, as described in our Privacy Policy.
