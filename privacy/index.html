Privacy Policy

A clear account of the information CompareZ uses to provide price comparison, meal planning, and AI-assisted shopping.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CompareZ collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the CompareZ mobile application and related services. “CompareZ,” “we,” and “our” refer to the CompareZ service operated by the developer identified on its Google Play listing.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password. Passwords are converted into a one-way cryptographic hash before storage; we do not store your readable password.

Location information

If you grant permission, CompareZ uses your location, city, and postal code to show locally relevant products, prices, and delivery areas. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings.

Shopping activity

We process product searches, comparison results, selected retailers, saved products, meal prompts, and ingredient lists to operate the service. Some preferences, including wishlist and recent-search data, may be stored locally on your device.

Connected retailer information

When you choose to connect a supported retailer, CompareZ may process the session information needed to retrieve results on your behalf. We do not use retailer credentials or session information to place orders. You can disconnect a retailer from the app.

BEANS and AI prompts

Text or audio you submit to BEANS, along with limited shopping context needed to answer your request, may be sent to an AI service provider for processing. Do not include payment information, passwords, or other sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

Technical information

We may process basic diagnostic data such as request identifiers, timestamps, error details, app version, and network information to secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How we use information

  • To register and authenticate your account.
  • To retrieve and organise comparison results.
  • To calculate price and quantity-based value indicators.
  • To provide location-relevant results when permitted.
  • To operate BEANS and meal-to-cart features.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate errors, and protect the service.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

4. How information is shared

CompareZ does not sell personal information. We may share limited data with infrastructure, database, security, and AI-processing providers acting on our behalf. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the service.

When you open a third-party retailer, that retailer processes your activity under its own privacy policy. CompareZ does not control the retailer’s checkout, fulfilment, or privacy practices.

5. Security

We use HTTPS, authenticated API access, password hashing, request limits, and restricted access controls designed to protect user data. No electronic system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and service data only for as long as reasonably needed to operate CompareZ, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent fraud. Local app data can also remain on your device until you clear it, sign out, remove it in the app, or uninstall the app.

You may request deletion of your CompareZ account and associated personal data from the app or through our account deletion page. We may retain limited information when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

7. Children

CompareZ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CompareZ develops. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date on this page and, where appropriate, providing notice in the app.
